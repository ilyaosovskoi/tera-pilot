# Market Research Notes

Working market-analysis documents that inform the product strategy. They describe the
AI coding-agent market landscape, the trust problem, and the competitive frame — they
do **not** make claims about Tera Pilot's own capabilities. Claims discipline applies
(see [`TERA_PILOT_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md`](../../TERA_PILOT_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md) §11).
